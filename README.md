## 📌 About Me

My journey began with the discipline of studying at an **Islamic Boarding School (Pesantren)** and continues today as a **Management student (S1 Manajemen)** at Universitas Muhammadiyah Tangerang. This background sparked my deep curiosity about how organizations operate and how digital systems can eliminate real-world friction.

As a builder driven to understand things from the ground up, I focus on crafting lightweight systems using **PHP Native with Custom MVC architectures**—diving deep into relational database design and core business logic rather than relying on bloated boilerplate templates.

Today, I leverage **Google Antigravity and AI** as powerful learning and execution partners. This empowers me to explore and iterate rapidly by breaking down real operational challenges, structuring clean database models, and building reliable, production-ready systems that deliver tangible value.
